Portfolio Checkup 🔒 Your files never leave your device

See how risky your portfolio really is, in plain English

Drop in the spreadsheets your bank or broker gives you: holdings, trade history, bank or credit-card statements. You'll get a risk check, "what if 2008 happened again", fees, cash runway and suggestions, all compared with today's economy.

  • Works with Schwab, Fidelity, Vanguard, E*TRADE, Merrill, JPMorgan, Robinhood, Webull and most banks (Excel or CSV)
  • Private: everything is calculated inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
  • Free. No account needed.

1 Add your files

Where to find them: in your broker's website look for Positions → Export / Download and History / Activity → Download; in your bank, Download transactions (CSV). Account numbers aren't needed, so you can delete those columns first.

2 About you (optional, makes suggestions fit you)

Educational information, not investment, tax or legal advice. Portfolio Checkup is not a registered investment adviser or broker-dealer and doesn't know your full situation. Results use simplified long-run assumptions and historical periods that may not repeat. Talk to a qualified, fiduciary adviser before making decisions.

Economic data: FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Spreadsheet reading: SheetJS. Your files are processed locally in your browser; this site has no upload endpoint.
